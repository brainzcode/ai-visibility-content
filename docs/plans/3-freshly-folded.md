# Freshly Folded: plan

Goal: get Freshly Folded into the Google map pack and into ChatGPT and Claude answers for its core San Diego searches. This builds on the 2027 doubling plan shown to Steve on Nov 6 (reviews from 141 to about 581 by October 2027, 4 to 6 links a month). Sources are bookmark numbers in [the bookmarks analysis](../twitter-bookmarks-analysis.md).

## Where it stands

- **Google reviews.** 141 at 4.4 stars (Nov 4). Pickup competitors have 545 to 853.
- **Map pack.** Absent on all 18 tracked keywords, checked from San Diego (tracker, Nov 22 to 23). The pack is mostly Bolt (110 reviews), 4th Ave Cleaners (288), DropMint (410) and Fresh & Clean (545).
- **Organic.** Page one for most core searches (positions 2 to 9).
- **AI Overviews.** One appeared on 14 of 18 keywords, and Freshly Folded is cited in 9 of them.
- **ChatGPT and Claude.** Each names Freshly Folded on 10 of about 18 keywords. They miss it on:
  - "wash and fold san diego"
  - "fluff and fold san diego"
  - "best laundry pickup and delivery service in san diego"
  - most "near me" versions
- **Sources the assistants use instead:** Yelp, MapQuest, ThreeBestRated, San Diego Lineup, and the national services (Poplin, Laundryheap, HappyNest).
- **Open with Steve:** whether customers are served at the La Mesa address, which decides whether the profile can show it.

Rules that apply to every step: next-day, never same-day; never "free" pickup or delivery; `Fresh10` is the only code; no customer stories or quotes; not a laundromat.

## Week 1

1. **Link the Business Profile to Google Analytics** [#429]. About 15 minutes; needs owner or manager access to the profile.
   - Done when GA4 shows profile calls and direction clicks.
2. **Tag the profile's website link.** Add `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`, so profile visits and `book_click`s show apart from other organic traffic in GA4.
   - This is the first piece of the order-source tracking already planned.
3. **Start the review ask after every delivery.** The plan already targets 15 new reviews in November.
   - Send one plain message with the direct review link to every customer, from whichever system sends the delivery-complete text.
   - Ask every customer, not only happy ones. Offer no reward. Script no wording.
   - Never pair the ask with `Fresh10` or any discount.
   - Count new reviews every Monday.
4. **Rewrite the services on the profile** [#235]. About an hour.
   - In Edit Services, list only what Freshly Folded actually sells: wash and fold, pickup and delivery, commercial laundry, towel service for salons, gyms and spas, vacation rental laundry, hotel linen, and dry cleaning pickup if offered.
   - Run the prompt from #235: "write SEO optimized service descriptions, under 300 characters, with the service and city in each one, and interview me about my business first so we can make it human."
   - Answer the interview with the facts in the project memory: $2.49/lb weekly, $2.69/lb on request, next-day, $49.99 minimum.
   - Keep the city in the descriptions, not in the service names.

## Week 2

5. **Complete the profile** [#476].
   - Compare the primary category with the four businesses that hold the pack.
   - Use the full 750-character description.
   - Set a weekly rhythm: one post, a few real photos, a reply to every review.
6. **Claim the profiles the assistants cite** (tracker data).
   - Make the name, phone, hours and service area identical on Yelp, MapQuest, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect.
   - Fix any wrong details you find. That's the "contest" pile.
7. **Earn the listicles** (tracker data).
   - ThreeBestRated is cited 13 times: submit Freshly Folded for review, but don't pay for placement.
   - San Diego Lineup is cited 10 times: send the editor a short factual note with the services and pricing.
   - Count each as one of the 4 links planned for November.
8. **Put the address question to Steve, with the new evidence.** Freshly Folded is in none of the 18 map packs checked from San Diego.
   - Google allows a visible address only if customers are served there during posted hours. If they aren't, the address stays hidden, and reviews and the profile carry the weight.

## Weeks 3 to 4

9. **Win the searches the assistants miss.** Run the tracker's page analysis on "wash and fold san diego" and "fluff and fold san diego".
   - Update the page Google shows for each: answer the question in the first two sentences, with the price and next-day turnaround.
10. **Add a key-facts table to the About page** [#155]. Use confirmed facts only:
    - family-owned by Liz and Steve Carlin
    - 28 years combined experience (never "28 years running Freshly Folded")
    - service area, hours, pricing, minimum order, detergents offered
    - booking link
11. **Weekly internal-linking pass** [#35]. About 30 minutes a week once set up.
    - Export three months of Search Console data.
    - For each page, the queries it ranks 1 to 10 for become its anchor text.
    - Add 3 to 5 links per page, in body text only.
    - Claude shows a table and waits for approval before editing. Keep the changelog.
    - The WordPress MCP plugin needs installing; you run that.
12. **Ask "How did you hear about us?"** at signup or in the first-order message [#261].
    - Include "ChatGPT or another AI assistant" as an option.
13. **Check ChatGPT Business Hub** [#27]. Claiming is open wherever ChatGPT is available. See whether a pickup and delivery service qualifies, and claim it if so.

## Every month

14. **Rerun the tracker and send Steve one page:**
    - reviews this month against the plan
    - map-pack presence
    - ChatGPT and Claude named on k of n keywords
    - AI Overview citations
    - what changed
15. **Next month's work** goes to the two or three searches that moved least.

## Don't

- **Don't encourage reviews with anything.** Not with offers that "stack reviews", and not with `Fresh10`.
- **Don't use a fake address or second profile.** No virtual office, no rented sign, no extra Business Profiles [#476].
- **Don't plant mentions.** Post nothing on Reddit unless it's openly as the owner answering a real question [#442].
