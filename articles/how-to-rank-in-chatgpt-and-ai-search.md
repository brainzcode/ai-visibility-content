# How to rank in ChatGPT and AI search: close your review gap

In November we asked Claude for "wash and fold san diego" while checking on a laundry pickup service we work with. Claude described the business well, down to details that only appear in its customers' reviews: the detergents it offers, its drying settings, the text updates on pickup day. Then it sent the reader to two competitors first. One had about three times as many Google reviews. The other had six times as many.

That one answer explains how to rank in ChatGPT, Claude and the other AI assistants when you run a local business. The AI already knew who this business was. It recommended the businesses with more people vouching for them.

Most advice sold as AI SEO, generative engine optimization or answer engine optimization starts with technical fixes: schema markup, llms.txt files, prompt lists. They have their place. For a plumber, a med spa or a laundry service, the thing that moves AI answers first is a steady flow of genuine reviews, starting with Google. We call the distance between your review count and the businesses AI names instead your **review gap**. Closing it is the job.

## What AI checks before it names a local business

When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini for a business near them, the assistant searches the web and builds an answer from what it finds: your website, your listings on sites like Google, Yelp and MapQuest, and what customers have written about you. Yext studied <a href="https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">6.8 million citations in AI answers</a> across Gemini, OpenAI's models and Perplexity, and found that 44% came from businesses' own websites and 42% from listings.

Reviews decide which of those businesses get named. Insites looked at <a href="https://insites.com/blog/do-google-reviews-affect-ai-recommendations-yes-heres-by-how-much/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">10,000 US local businesses in ChatGPT and Perplexity</a>. The ones both assistants surfaced averaged 133.4 Google reviews. The ones neither surfaced averaged 10.7. That's a correlation, and busier businesses do collect more reviews. Still, a gap of more than twelve to one is too wide to wave away.

Customers are already asking. In <a href="https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">BrightLocal's 2026 consumer survey</a>, use of ChatGPT and other AI tools for local recommendations rose from 6% to 45% in a year.

The AI finds you through your listings. It picks you through your reviews.

## Which reviews each AI reads

Not every assistant reads the same reviews, and this is where most advice gets vague.

**Google's AI reads Google reviews.** Ask Maps, the Gemini feature inside Google Maps, draws on more than 300 million places and reviews from over 500 million contributors, <a href="https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-immersive-navigation/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">by Google's own description</a>. For Google's AI, your Google Business Profile reviews are the input.

**ChatGPT reads Yelp.** In July 2026 Yelp agreed to <a href="https://martech.org/yelp-brings-reviews-and-local-leads-to-chatgpt/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">license its reviews, ratings, photos and business details to OpenAI</a>, and ChatGPT answers can now show them with Yelp's branding. There's no public deal giving ChatGPT direct access to Google reviews. Your Google rating can still reach it through other pages that repeat it.

Our own tracking points the same way. Across the 35 answers ChatGPT and Claude gave to 18 San Diego laundry searches we tracked this month, Yelp was cited as a source 25 times and MapQuest 29 times. Listing pages, star ratings and all, are a large part of what the assistants read.

Google reviews feed Google's AI. Yelp feeds ChatGPT. You need both, and you get them in different ways.

## How to get more Google reviews and get named by AI

### 1. Measure your review gap

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google for your main service in your city, the way a customer would. Write down every business named and its Google review count, then put yours beside them. The difference is your target. Our free [AI Visibility Checker](/tools/ai-visibility-checker) does the asking for you. If your Google Business Profile isn't fully set up yet, start with our [local SEO guide for small business owners](/blog/local-seo-guide-for-small-business-owners-rank-higher-on-google-in-30-days), or the trade guides to [local SEO for law firms](/blog/local-seo-for-law-firms) and [local SEO for med spas](/blog/local-seo-for-med-spas).

### 2. Ask every customer, at the moment the job is done

In your Google Business Profile, choose "Ask for reviews" to get your Google review link. Send it in the same message that confirms the job is finished, while the customer still remembers the service. Ask everyone, not only the customers you expect to be happy. Google's rules forbid asking only happy customers, and they forbid offering anything in return for a review.

The law is stricter than it used to be. The <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials" rel="noopener" target="_blank">FTC's rule on consumer reviews</a>, fully in force since October 2024, bans fake reviews (including AI-generated ones) and rewards offered for a positive review. The FTC can now seek civil penalties from businesses that knowingly break it.

### 3. Keep the pace steady

Twenty reviews in one week and none for three months looks like a campaign. One or two a week looks like a business people use. Make the review request part of how every job ends, not a project you run when sales are slow.

### 4. Answer every review, including the bad ones

Your replies sit right beside the reviews AI reads. A calm, specific answer to a complaint tells the next customer more about you than another five stars.

### 5. Claim your Yelp page, but don't ask for Yelp reviews

<a href="https://www.yelp-support.com/article/Don-t-Ask-for-Reviews?l=en_US" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Yelp's rules</a> tell businesses not to ask anyone for reviews, and its software filters out reviews it thinks were solicited. So claim the listing, fill in your services, hours and photos, and let reviews arrive on their own. With ChatGPT now reading Yelp, a complete page with a few honest reviews beats an empty one.

Then do the same on Bing Places and Apple Business Connect, with the same name, address, phone number and hours everywhere. Our [local business SEO maintenance checklist](/blog/complete-checklist-maintenance-local-business-seo-guide-2026) covers which listings to keep in step.

### 6. Put the facts on your website in plain words

List your services, prices, the areas you cover, your hours and how booking works, as text on the page rather than inside images or pop-ups. AI can only repeat what it can read. If your site hides these, a [rebuild focused on clear service pages](/services/web-development-services) or a few [single-purpose landing pages](/services/landing-pages) can fix it.

Reviews get you picked. Facts get you described correctly.

## The shortcuts that backfire

Bought reviews, AI-written reviews, review swaps with other businesses and "10% off for a five-star review" all break Google's rules, and most break the FTC's too. Google removes reviews that break its policies, and Yelp hides them from your rating.

A review gap closed with fake reviews reopens the day they're taken down.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ChatGPT use Google reviews?

There's no public deal giving ChatGPT direct access to Google reviews. ChatGPT licenses Yelp's reviews and searches the web, so a Google rating reaches it when another page repeats it. Google's own AI, including Ask Maps in Google Maps, reads Google reviews directly.

### How many reviews do I need to show up in ChatGPT?

There's no published threshold. In Insites' study of 10,000 US businesses, the ones ChatGPT and Perplexity both recommended averaged about 133 Google reviews. The number that matters for you is the count held by the businesses AI names for your service in your area.

### Can I offer a discount in exchange for reviews?

No. Google bans incentives for reviews of any kind, and the FTC's 2024 rule bans offering anything in exchange for a positive review. Ask every customer the same way and offer nothing for it.

### Should I ask customers for Yelp reviews too?

No. Yelp tells businesses not to ask anyone for reviews, and its software filters out reviews it thinks were solicited. Claim and complete your Yelp page and let those reviews come on their own.

### How long until AI starts recommending my business?

It depends on how many customers you serve, because reviews arrive at the pace of your jobs. AI answers also change from week to week, so check where you stand every month rather than once.
