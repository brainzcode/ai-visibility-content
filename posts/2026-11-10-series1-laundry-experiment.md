```
I asked AI for a wash and fold in San Diego. It skipped a business with a perfect 5-star rating.

It found 10 businesses and recommended 4.

Three things decided who made the cut.

1. Review volume
A 5-star rating from 2 reviews got a passing mention. 5 stars from nearly 1,900 reviews was treated as proof.

2. What the reviews say
The AI said one service lets you pick your detergent and drying settings. That detail came from customer reviews, not the business's website.

3. An address on file
Two highly rated pickup-and-delivery services had no public address. They never appeared on the map.

Three fixes you can make this week:

→ Ask every customer for a review within a day of the job. Every customer, not only the happy ones, and never offer anything in return.
→ Ask them to share what you did and how it went. Specific reviews give AI something to repeat.
→ No storefront? Set up as a service-area business in Google Business Profile and list the areas you cover. Your home address stays private.

Your customers can ask AI the same question about your business today.

Comment CHECK and we'll tell you whether AI recommends your business, free.
```
