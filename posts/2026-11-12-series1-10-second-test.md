## Master post (LinkedIn)

```
Open any AI assistant and type your service plus your city. Are you in the answer?

Do this before you finish your coffee.

Open ChatGPT, Gemini or Google and search the way a customer would:

"best [your service] in [your city]"

Then check three things.

1. Are you named at all? Read the whole answer, not only the top pick.
2. Is what it says about you right? Your hours, your services, the areas you cover, your phone number.
3. Who did it recommend instead, and what do they have that you don't?

When I ran this for wash and fold in San Diego, the AI found 10 businesses and recommended 4. Review volume, what the reviews said, and an address on file decided who made the cut.

Reply IN or OUT.

We'll go through the OUTs and tell you what's holding you back.
```
